# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 24 November 2026

JNT & Co ("we," "us," or "our") operates the ANIMA mobile application (the "App") and the website anima-app.net (the "Website"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

---

## 1. Who We Are

JNT & Co is the developer of the App and the data controller responsible for your personal data. We are based in Greece.

**Contact:** [support@anima-app.net](mailto:support@anima-app.net)

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
You can create an account in one of three ways:
- **Email and password.** We collect your email address and a securely hashed password. We send a one-time code to your email to confirm it. We never have access to your plaintext password.
- **Sign in with Apple.** Apple tells us a stable account identifier and your email address. You can choose Apple's "Hide My Email", in which case we only receive a private relay address that forwards to you. Apple may also share your name the first time you sign in; we keep it with your account. We never receive your Apple ID password.
- **Sign in with Google.** Google tells us your Google account identifier, email address and name. We never receive your Google password.

You may also choose a display name and an avatar. Authentication is handled by our backend provider, Supabase (Section 4.1).

### 2.2 Content You Provide
ANIMA offers several self-reflection practices. In the course of using any of them, you provide:
- **Intentions**: the personal question, theme, or (in some practices) the person you are reflecting on, set at the start of a session.
- **Reflection answers**: what you write in response to prompts during a session.

This content is, by the nature of the App, personal and may touch on sensitive subjects (thoughts, emotions, personal circumstances). We treat it accordingly. See Section 4.

### 2.3 Information About Other People
One of ANIMA's practices (Naikan) is specifically structured around reflecting on a real, named person in your life, for example a parent, partner, friend, or colleague. If you use this practice, the name and any details you write about that person are processed and stored the same way your own reflection content is.

**We do not independently collect, verify, or use information about anyone other than you.** Any information about a third party exists in our systems only because you chose to write it as part of your own reflection. Please be thoughtful about what you share regarding other people. Avoid including information that is unnecessarily identifying or sensitive about them, and be aware that this content is processed by our AI service provider (see Section 4.2) the same way your own content is.

### 2.3a Automatic Safety Check
Your intention and every answer you write are checked automatically, on your phone and on our server, for words and phrases about suicide or self-harm. If such a phrase is found, **your text is not sent to the AI and that answer is not saved.** Instead, the App shows a Help card with buttons to call emergency services or a free helpline, and a link to the Calm screen. Tapping a call button only opens your phone's dialer with the number filled in; you decide whether to call.

To show the right numbers, the App uses your phone's region setting. If you have already allowed location access (Section 2.4), it may instead use your approximate location, turned into a country by your phone's own operating system (Apple or Google). The country is used only on your phone to choose which numbers to show; it is not stored or sent to us.

This check is fully automatic. **No person reads your text because of it, no one is notified or contacted, and we do not create any flag, score, or record about you from it.** It is a keyword check, so it can miss some messages and occasionally match one that was not about harm. It is not a crisis service; see Section 10.

### 2.3b Staying With the Practice
When you start a practice, your intention is sent to the AI service (Section 4.2) for a quick check that it is about your own life (or, in Naikan, a person in your life). If it is not, for example a maths question or a request for other content, the App gently asks you to rewrite it. During a practice, if the AI finds that an answer is not a reflection at all, it gives a short, gentle reply and **that answer is not saved**; the practice does not move on until you answer again. These checks are automatic and nothing about them is recorded about you.

### 2.4 Approximate Location (Optional)
If you allow location access, the App reads your **approximate** location (city or region level, not precise GPS) to show the current local weather and time of day in the App's sky. The coordinates are sent to our weather provider (see Section 4.3) to get the forecast. **We do not store your location** on our servers or link it to your account. If you decline location access, the App works normally with a default sky.

### 2.5 Gameplay Data
We record activity needed to run the App, including session progress, timestamps, which parts of a practice you have engaged with, rewards and milestones you have reached, and which session was your free session with AI reflections (Section 2.9).

### 2.6 Information Stored Only on Your Device
Some settings are kept only on your phone and are never sent to us: your language, sound, cat voice and haptics preferences, graphics settings, onboarding progress, your progress in the Sky puzzles, whether usage statistics are on (Section 2.8), and the practice reminders you schedule. Reminders are local notifications created on your device. We do not use push notification services to contact you.

### 2.6a Calm Mode
The Calm screen (guided breathing, the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise, and petting the cat) works entirely on your phone; you can open it from Home, from the Help card, or from your phone's shortcuts. It can be opened without an account, sends nothing to us or to the AI, and saves nothing. The Android Quick Settings tile and app-icon shortcut, and any iPhone Shortcut or Back Tap you set up for it, only open this screen.

### 2.7 Crash Reports
If the App crashes or hits an error, a crash report is sent so we can fix the problem. It contains technical details only: the error and where in the App's code it happened, your device model and operating system version, the App version, and the screens and network requests that led up to it. Crash reports never include what you write in the App, your email address, your name, or your IP address. See Section 4.5.

### 2.8 Usage Statistics
To understand how the App is used and where it can be improved, the App records simple usage events, for example: the App was opened, onboarding was started or finished, a practice was chosen, started, finished or left, a step of a practice was completed, the journal was opened, a reward was given, the AI's reply was shown (with how long it took and how long it was) or failed, the subscription screen was shown or closed, a plan was chosen, a trial or purchase started, failed or was restored, and the store review request was shown.

Each event carries only: the event's name and time, the App version, your platform (iOS or Android), your country (from your phone's region setting, or from the network your request comes through), a random installation identifier created by the App, the paywall group your installation is in (Section 2.10), and a few related numbers or labels (such as which practice, which step, or which plan). When you are signed in, events are linked to your account.

**Usage events never contain what you write**: not your intentions, answers, names of other people, or the AI's replies. These statistics are collected by our own system (stored with Supabase, Section 4.1). We do not use third-party analytics or advertising tools, and we do not use your phone's advertising identifier.

You can turn usage statistics off at any time in **Settings → Usage statistics**. When you do, the App stops recording them and deletes any events it has not sent yet.

### 2.9 Subscription and Purchase Information
ANIMA is free to use, with one full session including the AI facilitator's reflections. The paid plan, **Anima+**, adds the facilitator's reflections to every session. If you buy or start a trial of Anima+:
- Payment is handled entirely by **Apple (App Store)** or **Google (Google Play)**. We never see or store your card or bank details.
- We receive, through our subscription provider RevenueCat (Section 4.6), information about your subscription: the plan, its status (trial, active, cancelled, expired, refunded, billing problem), renewal and end dates, the price and currency, the store and the store's country. It is linked to your ANIMA account so the App knows you have Anima+ on every device.
- Our team can also give Anima+ to an account by hand (for example to testers or as a thank-you); we record who gave it, why and until when.

### 2.10 Pricing Experiment (Paywall Groups)
To learn when it is most helpful to show the Anima+ subscription screen, each installation of the App is placed at random, once, into one of a few groups. The group decides only *when* the subscription screen first appears (for example, after your first finished practice, or before onboarding). It does not change the price, what is free, or what Anima+ includes. We store the group with your installation identifier, your platform, App version and country, and link it to your account when you sign in.

### 2.11 AI Usage Records
For every request to the AI service, our server records technical information only: which practice, the AI model used, how many units of text ("tokens") were sent and received, how long it took, and, if it failed, the kind of error. **These records never contain your text or the AI's reply.** We use them to keep the AI service working, to find failures, and to understand its cost.

### 2.12 Support Requests
If you contact us:
- **From the App** (Settings → Support): we store your message and our replies, the topic you chose, your account's email address, and your App version, platform and operating system version, so we can help with the right details.
- **From the Website's contact form**: we store your email address, the name you give (optional), your message and the topic. To prevent abuse, we keep a one-way scrambled code made from your IP address (not the IP address itself) for a limited count of requests per day.
- **By email** to support@anima-app.net: we receive your email and anything you write.

Replies are sent to your email address through our email provider, Resend (Section 4.7), and, for requests from the App, also shown in the App. Please do not include your reflections or other private notes in support requests; they are not needed to help you.

### 2.13 Store Review Request
After you have finished a few practices, the App may ask Apple or Google to show their standard "rate this app" prompt, once. Apple or Google decide whether it actually appears; any rating or review you give goes to them, not to us. We only record that the request was made (Section 2.8).

### 2.14 Information We Do Not Collect
We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. We do not use advertising, attribution or third-party analytics tools, and the App does not display ads. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:
- Create and maintain your account, including signing in with Apple or Google
- Save your progress across sessions and devices
- Generate the AI facilitator's reflections and questions in response to what you write (see Section 4)
- Show emergency and helpline information instead of an AI response when what you write mentions suicide or self-harm (see Section 2.3a)
- Check that intentions and answers stay with the practice (see Section 2.3b)
- Provide Anima+ and know whether your subscription is active (Section 2.9)
- Understand how the App is used and improve it, including the pricing experiment (Sections 2.8 and 2.10)
- Keep the AI service working and understand its cost (Section 2.11)
- Answer your support requests (Section 2.12)
- Show local weather in the App, if you allow location access
- Maintain the security and integrity of the App, and prevent abuse
- Diagnose and fix technical problems, including through crash reports
- Keep the records the law requires (for example for payments)

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your reflection content for advertising, analytics, or to train AI models.

### Who at ANIMA can see your information
A small number of team members have access to an internal administration panel. It shows account details (email address, sign-up date, country, platform, paywall group), subscription status, counts and dates of sessions, AI usage costs, and support requests. **The panel does not show your intentions, reflection answers, or the AI's replies.** Each team member's access is limited to what their role needs, and changes made in the panel are logged.

### Legal bases (for users in the EU, EEA and UK)
- **Performance of our contract with you**: your account (including Apple and Google sign-in), your content, the AI facilitator, Anima+ and answering your support requests are the service you signed up for.
- **Consent**: approximate location, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings.
- **Legitimate interests**: keeping the App secure and preventing abuse; fixing technical problems; usage statistics and the pricing experiment, to understand and improve the App and keep it sustainable; AI usage records. You can object to these at any time, and you can turn usage statistics off in Settings (Section 2.8).
- **Legal obligation**: keeping payment-related records where the law requires it.
- **Vital interests and your safety**: the automatic safety check (Section 2.3a), which exists only to point you to real help.

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on the following service providers to operate the App. Each processes a limited set of your data only to provide their service to us. None of them may use your data for their own purposes.

### 4.1 Supabase (Database, Authentication, Hosting)
Supabase stores your account information, session history, reflection content, subscription status, usage statistics, AI usage records and support requests in a secured database, and runs our server functions. Access to your content is restricted so that only your own account can read or write it (enforced with row-level security).

### 4.2 OpenLux and OpenAI (AI Facilitator)
When you submit a reflection answer, the relevant text (your stated intention, the current prompt, and your answer) is sent to an AI model to generate the facilitator's response. Your intention is also sent once, when you start a practice, for the check described in Section 2.3b. This applies across every practice in the App, including content that names or describes another person (see Section 2.3). If you have chosen a display name, it is included so the facilitator can occasionally address you by it. Your email address and password are never sent. The exchange happens through a secured server-side connection.

The request is sent through OpenLux, an AI gateway service, which passes it to a language model made by OpenAI and returns the response. OpenLux and OpenAI process this text only to generate the response. We ask that these exchanges are not stored for later use.

If you have concerns about sensitive content being processed by an AI system, including content about people other than yourself, please consider this before writing particularly sensitive personal details into your reflections.

### 4.3 Open-Meteo (Weather)
If you allow location access, your approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the local weather forecast. No account information or reflection content is sent to Open-Meteo.

### 4.4 Apple and Google
The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play, which also process subscription payments, and you can sign in with your Apple or Google account. Apple and Google process that information under their own privacy policies ([Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

### 4.5 Sentry (Crash Reports)
Crash reports (Section 2.7) are processed by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so we can see and fix errors. Sentry receives only the technical details listed in Section 2.7, never your reflections or account details, and deletes them automatically after its retention period (at most 90 days).

### 4.6 RevenueCat (Subscriptions)
RevenueCat, Inc. connects the App to Apple's and Google's purchase systems and tells our server when a subscription starts, renews, is cancelled, refunded or expires (Section 2.9). It receives your ANIMA account identifier and the purchase information from the store. It does not receive your reflections, your email address or your payment details.

### 4.7 Resend (Email)
Resend, Inc. sends our support emails: confirmations that we received your request and our replies (Section 2.12). It receives your email address and the content of those emails. Verification codes for email sign-up are sent by Supabase.

### 4.8 GitHub (Website Hosting)
The Website (including this policy, the support page and its contact form) is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit. Messages sent through the contact form go directly to our Supabase server, not to GitHub.

## 5. Data Retention

- **Account, content and progress:** kept for as long as your account remains active.
- **Subscription information:** kept while your account exists. Payment records themselves are kept by Apple or Google, under their own policies.
- **Usage statistics and AI usage records:** kept for as long as they are useful for understanding and improving the App and its costs. When you delete your account, they are separated from your account (the link to you is removed).
- **Support requests from the App:** kept until you delete your account.
- **Support requests from the Website:** kept for as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up, and deleted on request.
- **Crash reports:** at most 90 days (Section 4.5).

If you delete your account, your account and all associated data (intentions, reflections, session history, milestones, support requests from the App, subscription status and any access given by hand) are permanently deleted from our database, except where retention is required by law. Copies in routine backups are overwritten within a limited period afterwards.

## 6. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time:
- **In the App:** Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and everything in it. If you signed in with Apple, the App asks Apple to confirm once, so that we can also cancel ANIMA's access to your Apple ID.
- **Without the App:** email [support@anima-app.net](mailto:support@anima-app.net) from the address you signed up with.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active Anima+ subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings, or you may keep being charged.

See [Delete your account](/delete-account/) for the details.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and associated data
- Export your data in a portable format
- Object to or restrict certain processing (including usage statistics and the pricing experiment)
- Withdraw consent you have given (for example, location access)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [support@anima-app.net](mailto:support@anima-app.net). We will respond within one month.

If you are in the EU or EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Greece, this is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority ([dpa.gr](https://www.dpa.gr)).

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the App and our servers, database-level access controls that isolate each user's data from every other user's, and role-based access with logging for our team's administration panel. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Not a Substitute for Professional Care

ANIMA is a self-reflection tool designed to support awareness and self-observation. It is not therapy, medical advice, or a substitute for professional mental health treatment. If you are experiencing a mental health crisis, please contact a licensed professional or a crisis service in your area. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU and Ukraine). Free helplines include 1018 in Greece and 7333 (Lifeline Ukraine); for other countries, see findahelpline.com. The Calm screen and the App's safety message are there to help in the moment; they are not an emergency or crisis service.
