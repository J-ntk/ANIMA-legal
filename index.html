ANIMA

Inner Reflection

A quiet space for self-inquiry, reflection, and awareness. Four contemplative practices, each rooted in a real tradition. There is no score and no winning. Only what you notice.

The practices

  • Leela: the ancient Indian game of self-knowledge, played on a 3D board.
  • The Great Work: a staged practice of deliberate inner work.
  • The Labyrinth: a slow, meditative walk toward the centre.
  • Naikan: the Japanese method of reflecting on what you have received from others.

In each practice, an AI facilitator reflects patterns back to you and asks one question. It does not give advice, predict your future, or tell you what to do.

ANIMA is not therapy and is not a substitute for professional care.
